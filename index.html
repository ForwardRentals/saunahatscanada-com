How it works

From logo to hat in weeks

  1. Send your design and quantity. Logo, name or artwork, plus roughly how many you need.
  2. Get a quote and mock-up. We come back with pricing, a mock-up and a timeline, usually within one business day.
  3. Approve and we embroider. Your design goes on the same thick wool hats sold in the Sauna Hats Canada shop.
  4. Delivered across Canada. Shipped from Squamish, BC to your door.

FAQ

Custom & wholesale questions

What's the minimum order?

Typical custom runs start around 200 pieces. One-off custom hats are possible on request at a higher per-hat price, from $59.99 CAD.

What can you embroider?

Logos, names, artwork and text. Send your file and we'll mock it up on the hat before anything is made.

Do you sell plain hats wholesale?

Yes. Saunas, studios and shops can stock plain wool and merino sauna hats with volume pricing. Ask for a wholesale quote.
